Advisory

Practical delivery support when projects need structure.

I support SaaS companies, PMO teams and project leaders who need practical help with delivery governance, project recovery, stakeholder alignment or AI-enabled project workflows.

I step in as a project or delivery lead to take ownership of a project, bring structure to the work, align stakeholders, manage risks and keep execution moving toward clear outcomes.

When I Can Help

When delivery needs stronger leadership

  • A project is delayed and the next steps are unclear.
  • Leadership does not have enough visibility into project health.
  • Risks are known, but not managed consistently.
  • Stakeholders are misaligned or escalation paths are unclear.
  • The PMO needs better reporting, governance or delivery rhythm.
  • Teams want to use AI in project work but need practical guardrails.

Advisory Focus Areas

Four ways I can support delivery teams.

PMO & Delivery Governance

Lightweight governance, reporting, risk visibility and decision structure.

Project Recovery

Short diagnostic and recovery planning for delayed or unclear projects.

Stakeholder & Escalation Management

Support with difficult stakeholder environments, executive updates and escalation rhythm.

AI-Enabled Project Workflows

Practical use of AI for reporting, risk detection, meeting follow-up and delivery governance.
